How the Placement Portal Operates

A transparent 4-stage recruitment pipeline from day one of college enrollment to offer letter handover.

1
Profile & Resume Setup

Students create profiles with verified academic scores, technical skills, and upload standard PDF resumes.

2
Skill Bootcamps

Enrolling in hands-on industry training programs on algorithms, full-stack development, and behavioral interview coaching.

3
Drive Applications

Browse company openings, verify automatic eligibility, apply with one click, and track real-time shortlisting stages.

4
Selection & Placement

Recruiters shortlist, schedule interviews, and select candidates. Successful hires are recorded into college placement history.
